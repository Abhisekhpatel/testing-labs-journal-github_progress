## 📘 1. Summary of the Task
Describe the purpose of this activity in your own words:  
- What is the goal of the lab?  
- What network are you expected to create?

The purpose of this activity is to learn how to create and configure a basic computer network. The goal of the lab is to connect devices, assign IP addresses, and test communication between them. In this lab, we are expected to build a small working network and understand how devices communicate with each other.
