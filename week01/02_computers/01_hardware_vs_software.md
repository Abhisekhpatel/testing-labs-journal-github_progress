## Explain Software
Software is the invisible layer of a computer — a collection of coded instructions that tell the hardware exactly what to do and when to do it. Unlike hardware, you cannot touch software; it exists purely as data. It comes in two main forms: system software, such as Windows, macOS, or Linux, which manages the hardware and keeps everything running smoothly behind the scenes, and application software, like web browsers, word processors, games, and streaming apps, which lets users carry out specific tasks. Every tap on your phone, every website you load, every document you type — all of it is driven by software working quietly underneath.
